About MTR Trading

Your trusted partner for quality mattresses in Rwanda

Our Story

MTR Trading Ltd has been serving Rwandan families with quality mattresses for years. We understand that a good night's sleep is essential for health, productivity, and overall wellbeing. That's why we are committed to providing mattresses that combine comfort, durability, and affordability.

Starting from a small shop in Kigali, we have grown to become a trusted name in the bedding industry. Our success comes from our dedication to quality products and exceptional customer service.

Our Mission

To provide every Rwandan family with access to high-quality mattresses that promote better sleep and healthier lives, at prices that are fair and affordable.

  • Quality materials and craftsmanship
  • Affordable prices for every budget
  • Excellent customer service
  • Fast and reliable delivery
  • 100% customer satisfaction
