What Does Warranty Cover?

  • ✓ Manufacturing defects
  • ✓ Excessive sagging (more than 1.5 inches)
  • ✓ Broken or loose springs (for spring mattresses)
  • ✓ Faulty zippers on removable covers
  • ✓ Deterioration of foam quality
What is NOT Covered:
  • Normal wear and tear
  • Stains, burns, or physical damage
  • Discomfort or preference issues
  • Damage from improper use or handling
  • Mattress without original proof of purchase
How to Make a Warranty Claim:
  1. Contact us via WhatsApp or phone with your order details
  2. Provide photos showing the issue
  3. Share your proof of purchase (order ID)
  4. Our team will inspect and verify within 48 hours
  5. If approved, we will repair or replace your mattress
