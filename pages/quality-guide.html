How to identify a quality mattress - Your complete guide

What Makes a Quality Mattress?

Before buying a mattress, it's important to know what to look for. Here are the key factors that determine mattress quality.

Density

High density foam (30kg/m³+) lasts longer and provides better support. Low density foam will sag quickly.

Firmness (Ubukomere)

Choose based on your sleeping position. Side sleepers need softer, back sleepers need firmer.

Durability (Iramba)

A quality mattress should last over 12 years. Check warranty period as an indicator.

Breathability (Umwuka)

Good air circulation prevents heat buildup and keeps you cool while sleeping.

Quality Checklist

Use this checklist when evaluating a mattress:

  • Check foam density (ask the seller for specifications)
  • Press down on the mattress - it should spring back quickly
  • Look for a removable, washable cover
  • Check the warranty period (minimum 1-2 years for good quality)
  • Feel the edges - they should be firm and supportive
  • Lie down for 5-10 minutes to test comfort
  • Check for any chemical smells (off-gassing)
  • Ask about the spring count for spring mattresses

Mattress Types Comparison

Foam Mattress

Pros: Affordable, good support, quiet
Cons: Can retain heat
Best for: Budget-conscious buyers

Spring Mattress

Pros: Bouncy, good airflow
Cons: Can be noisy, less motion isolation
Best for: Couples, hot sleepers

Orthopaedic Mattress

Pros: Excellent back support, firm
Cons: Can be too firm for some
Best for: Back pain sufferers

Pro Tip:

Always test a mattress for at least 10 minutes in the store. Lie in your normal sleeping position. A quality mattress should feel comfortable immediately - your body shouldn't have to "adjust" to it.

Questions to Ask Before Buying

  • What is the foam density?
  • How long is the warranty?
  • Is there a trial period?
  • What is the return policy?
  • Is delivery free?
  • What payment methods are accepted?
