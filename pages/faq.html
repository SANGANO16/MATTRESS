Frequently Asked Questions - Find answers to common questions

About Our Mattresses

What types of mattresses do you sell?

We sell Plain Mattress (PF), Lala Salama (LS), Sweet Dreams (SD), Prestige Quilted (PQ), Five Star Spring, and Ortho-Medic (OM). Each type has different features and price points to suit various needs and budgets.

What sizes are available?

We offer Single (190x90 cm), Double (190x120 cm), Queen (190x160 cm), and King (200x180 cm). Custom sizes available on request.

How do I choose the right mattress firmness?

Side sleepers prefer softer mattresses, back sleepers need medium-firm, and stomach sleepers require firm mattresses. Visit our quality guide for more details.

Orders & Payment

How do I place an order?

Click "Yitumize Nonaha!" on any product, fill in your details, send the 5,000 Rwf deposit via MTN or Airtel Money, and we'll confirm your order within 30 minutes.

Why do I need to pay a deposit?

The 5,000 Rwf deposit confirms your serious intent, reserves your mattress, and covers delivery preparation. This amount is deducted from the total price.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, Cash on Delivery (remaining balance), and Bank Transfer.

Is my deposit refundable?

Yes, your deposit is 100% refundable if we cannot deliver your mattress. Refunds are processed within 48 hours.

Delivery Information

How long does delivery take?

Delivery takes 1-3 days after payment confirmation. Priority orders (with deposit) are delivered within 24 hours in Kigali.

Do you offer free delivery?

Yes, delivery is free within Kigali city limits. For other locations, delivery fee is calculated based on distance.

Do you deliver outside Kigali?

Yes, we deliver to all districts of Rwanda. Contact us for delivery fees to your specific location.

Warranty & Returns

What is your warranty policy?

Warranty varies by product: 1 year for Plain Mattress, 2 years for Lala Salama, 3 years for Sweet Dreams, and 5 years for Prestige Quilted.

Can I return my mattress?

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee for unused mattresses in original condition. Contact us within 7 days of delivery to initiate return.
